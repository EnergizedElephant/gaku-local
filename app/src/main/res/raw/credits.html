Gaku

Gaku is open source, under the BSD 3-Clause licence below. Its source code is at github.com/EnergizedElephant/gaku-local.

It's based on Kaku by 0xbad1d3a5 and Gaku by Carl Oosthuizen. This version was made with Claude Code, Anthropic's AI coding assistant.

Dictionary

This app uses the JMdict/EDICT and KANJIDIC dictionary files. These files are the property of the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group, and are used in conformance with the Group's licence.

Gaku's dictionary database is built from these files, and is likewise available under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence.

Deinflection rules

The rules that find the dictionary form of a conjugated word (deinflect.dat) are by Jonathan Zarate, from Rikaichan, under the GNU General Public License, version 2 or later, below.

Similar kanji

The lookalike kanji offered for a misread character are derived from Lars Yencken's kanji confusion data (Yencken and Baldwin, 2008), under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 licence.

Reading text

manga-ocr's model and vocabulary are by Maciej Budyś, under the Apache License 2.0 below. Gaku uses the int8 ONNX export from onnx-community/manga-ocr-base-ONNX.

ONNX Runtime, which runs the model, is by Microsoft, under the MIT licence below. Its third-party notices are at the end.

Google ML Kit text recognition, with the Google Play services libraries it uses, is used under the ML Kit Terms of Service and the Android Software Development Kit License. It isn't open source. When you use Read with ML Kit, ML Kit can send Google usage metrics, such as how long a read took, through Google Play services. It doesn't send your text or images.
